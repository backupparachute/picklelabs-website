What Makes the Fuse Different

Two pieces of tech carry this paddle

DuraSpin Surface

Most gritty paddles buy their spin with a rough face that sands itself smooth over a season. DuraSpin goes after dynamic friction instead of raw abrasion, so the face grabs the ball through contact and holds that texture much longer.

  • Dry, connected feel on contact
  • Texture that survives real play, not just the first month
  • Heavy topspin on serves, drives, and roll volleys

TriFusion All-Foam Core

Three foams bonded into one core: an MPP center, a high-rebound TPU band, and an EVA outer band. The result plays stiff and lively, with energy coming back at you rather than dying in the panel.

  • Ball jumps off the face on counters and overheads
  • Wide, even sweet spot
  • Pace without having to swing out of your shoes
